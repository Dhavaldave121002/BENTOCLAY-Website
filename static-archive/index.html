01 / OUR MINERAL

One remarkable clay.
Countless possibilities.

Attapulgite is a naturally occurring hydrated aluminium magnesium silicate. Its unique rod-like structure delivers excellent absorption, suspension, binding and rheological performance.

At Bentoclay Claytech, we process this versatile mineral into carefully controlled grades built for real-world industrial performance.
