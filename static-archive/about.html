OUR MATERIAL

Nature’s structure,
refined for industry.

Attapulgite is a naturally occurring hydrous magnesium aluminosilicate with a distinctive rod-like crystal structure. Accessory minerals may include quartz, feldspar and calcite. This structure gives the mineral valuable absorption, suspension and binding properties.

Our processing focuses on drying and grinding selected clay to controlled particle-size distributions and performance parameters. The result is a versatile portfolio serving drilling, foundry, construction, agriculture and chemical applications.
